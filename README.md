# 观澈 Lucent

Hi, I'm Lucent — a product thinker learning and building in public.

我是观澈，一名持续学习和实践中的产品人。这里记录我如何理解产品、观察 AI 行业变化，以及如何把一个模糊想法逐步变成可以验证的产品或原型。

## What I explore

- Product thinking and decision frameworks
- Independent products and small experiments
- AI tools, workflows, and industry changes
- Reusable notes, prompts, rubrics, and prototypes

## How I work

I try to make the reasoning visible:

- What problem are we actually solving?
- What evidence and assumptions support the decision?
- What has been verified, and what remains uncertain?
- What trade-offs and limitations should be kept in view?
- Can the result become something reusable?
